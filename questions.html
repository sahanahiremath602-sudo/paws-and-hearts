🏡 About Pet Adoption

Pet adoption means welcoming an animal into your family and taking responsibility for its care. Instead of purchasing a pet, you provide a home to an animal that needs one.

Adoption gives an animal an opportunity to live in a safe and caring environment. It also encourages responsible pet ownership and helps raise awareness about animal welfare.

Yes! Beginners can adopt a pet if they understand its needs and are ready to provide food, shelter, exercise, veterinary care, patience and affection. Choosing a pet whose needs match your lifestyle is important.

Consider your daily schedule, available space, budget, experience and the animal's temperament. Puppies and kittens often require considerable supervision, while adult animals may have more established personalities.

Absolutely! Paws & Hearts features both cats and dogs. Cats need appropriate food, clean litter, safe surroundings, enrichment and veterinary care. Dogs also need exercise, training, companionship and suitable daily activities.

🥣 Pet Care and Responsibilities

Prepare suitable food, fresh water, a safe sleeping area, appropriate bowls, cleaning supplies and necessary enrichment. Cats also need litter facilities, while dogs need suitable exercise and training. Arrange veterinary care before bringing your new companion home.

The schedule depends on the animal's age, health and medical history. Puppies and kittens need age-appropriate preventive care, while adult animals also benefit from routine check-ups. Ask a veterinarian to recommend an appropriate schedule.

Give your pet a quiet, safe space and allow it time to adjust. Use gentle handling, predictable routines and positive reinforcement. Avoid forcing interactions, and consult a veterinarian or qualified behaviour professional if the fear is severe or persistent.
